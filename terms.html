Last updated: 8 November 2026

What you are buying

Server Tool Pro is a one-time software license, not a subscription. A valid license key unlocks the Pro features of the Server Tool Pro Windows application for the 1.x release line. The free edition of Server Tool already lets you create, run, monitor and manually back up one Paper or Vanilla server without a key.

Trial

The Pro installer includes a one-time seven-day trial that unlocks every Pro feature. The trial needs no payment method or account. After it ends, Pro features require a license key. The trial cannot be restarted, reset or transferred.

License scope

A license is issued to one person for personal or commercial use on servers that person operates. It is not tied to a specific computer, so you may move it to a replacement PC. You may install the app on more than one PC that you personally use, but you may not share the key publicly, resell it, sublicense it, or use one key for multiple people or teams.

Offline activation

License keys are digitally signed and verified locally by the application. Activation does not contact an activation server and does not require an internet connection. The key is stored on your computer. Removing the application's data folder or deactivating the key removes that local copy, after which the same key can be activated again.

Delivery and payment

Orders are handled manually. After payment is confirmed, a license key is sent to the email address provided during the order. Payment methods and the final total are confirmed in the order conversation before payment. The launch price shown on the Pro page may change for future customers; it does not change the price you already agreed to.

Refunds

Because a license key is a digital product delivered immediately, purchases are final once the key has been delivered. If a key does not activate or you were charged incorrectly, contact us and we will investigate and, where appropriate, reissue the key or refund the purchase. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under applicable consumer law.

Updates

Version 1.x updates are included with a Pro license. A future major version may require a new license; if that happens, existing customers will be offered a clearly discounted upgrade. You are responsible for keeping your own backups before updating.

Do not attempt to bypass, extract, share or resell license keys. We may revoke a license obtained through fraud, chargeback abuse or unauthorized resale.
