Account

Your orders.

Sign in to see the Pro orders you placed while signed in, and open a fresh claim link for a paid order. Buying never requires an account — this page is only a shortcut.

Accounts are not open yet. Sign-in switches on when the checkout service is deployed. Until then every order is handled by message and your key is sent to the email address on the order.

Use at least 10 characters. A password manager works well here.

What an account adds

  • Order history for purchases made while signed in
  • A fresh claim link without digging through old messages
  • The same offline licence — the app never needs an account

Orders placed before you created an account are not imported automatically, because this service does not verify email addresses yet. Keep the claim link from your purchase message, or look the order up with its token.

How keys are delivered

A paid order shows a claim button. The link is signed, tied to that one order and expires after a few days; open this page again for a new one.

This page never displays the licence key itself. Paste the key into Server Tool Pro once and it stays valid offline.

Payment questions, refunds or a key that will not activate? Open an issue with the order id.
